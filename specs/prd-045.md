# PRD 045 requirements: a groom ends every issue ready or with a stated reason

The format is `specs/utility-skills.md`'s: each row makes one claim a reviewer, a grader or a test
can mark true or false; IDs are stable, and a dropped row leaves a gap. A row whose ID is an existing
one rewords that row in place (written `<ID><reword>` in an issue body); a new row takes its
prefix's next free number.

**Status (2026-11-26): D1–D7 are the owner's answers of 2026-11-25. 045.1's rows are written against
`main` at `25701f92cc`.**

## The goal

`/vgroom` with no argument should leave every open issue either ready to develop (opted in) or
carrying one valid reason it is not, and the human gets one report listing those reasons. Today the
sweep reports drafts and skips `taken:` issues, but nothing bounds why an issue may stay unready,
parked and bailed issues get no special look, a draft whose reason lapsed is only named
"promotable", `--refactor-owed` is a flag although the sweep already runs it, and an issue is
labelled ready without `/vfile`'s input check.

**Measured** at `origin/main` 4bbd413a80, 2026-11-25 22:45 CST: 16 open issues, 7 solvable; 3
`taken:main`, 2 `draft`, 2 not opted in; 0 `parked`, `bailed`, `refactor-owed` or `candidate`.

**Target, in prose and forced by no criterion:** after a no-argument groom, every open issue that is
not solvable appears in the report's table with a reason from D2's list.

## 045.1: the closed reason list and the report table

The human gets: one table at the end of every no-argument groom naming each unready issue and its
one reason, and no issue left unready for a reason outside the list. Depends on nothing.

## Outlines (no rows yet)

### 045.2: the draft plan offer

A draft whose `## Not ready` condition no longer holds is offered `/vplan` in the groom; on a yes the
plan runs, on a no or no answer its reason is *not planned now*. Amends GR21. Blocked by 045.1.

### 045.3: parked and bailed revisit

The sweep reads each `parked` or `bailed` issue's cause; when the fix it waited on has landed
(a named issue closed, a named PR merged), it removes the label and grooms the issue as any other;
a park waiting on a human answer stays, reason *needs-human*. Blocked by 045.1.

### 045.4: drop `--refactor-owed`

`/vgroom` takes no `--refactor-owed`; the section becomes the sweep's refactor-owed step. GR1, GR3,
GR5, GR9, GR13, GR18–GR20 reworded; CLAUDE.md's `/vgroom --refactor-owed` and its CM80 row fixed in
the same change. Blocked by 045.1 (both reword GR13).

### 045.5: the input check before ready

`## Before labelling ready` also runs `/vfile`'s *Before filing* check 2 through a subagent; a failure
makes the issue `needs-rewrite`. Amends GR10. Blocked by 045.1 (same section's outcomes).
