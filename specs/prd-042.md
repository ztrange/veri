# PRD 042 requirements: slim veri's project CLAUDE.md

The format is `specs/utility-skills.md`'s: each row makes one claim a reviewer, a grader or a test
can mark true or false; IDs are stable, and a dropped row leaves a gap.

**Status (2026-11-20): written against `main` at `f424164952`. D1, D2 and D4 are the owner's
answers of 2026-11-20; D3 is open. Rows exist for 042.1 only; 042.2 and 042.3 are outlines.**

## The goal

Every leaf and the conductor load veri's root `CLAUDE.md` on every request, so every byte in it is
paid once per request of every session that runs in this repository. Its *Testing idiom* section
has doubled in nine days, almost all of it the pytest leg's machinery (the workers budget, the
locks, the bytecode cache), which a leaf writing a test never needs and which the code and its
tests already hold.

**Target, in prose and forced by no criterion:** fewer bytes loaded per leaf request. 042.1 is
expected to take about 10 KB out of the *Testing idiom* (paragraphs 3 and 4 of the section are
9,963 B at `f424164952`, of which the case timeouts, the admission rule and the leg's launch stay),
bringing the file back near 19 KB. The criteria below are the behaviours that force it.

- **Moved rows keep their IDs** (`CM54`, `CAP8`, …) under the README's heading, rather than being

  `f424164952` it is not (see Facts), so D2's "under the README's unit" means creating it.

## 042.2 (outline): a test caps `CLAUDE.md`'s bytes

**What the human gets:** the file cannot quietly grow back. Blocked by 042.1. A fast-tier case
fails when the root `CLAUDE.md` exceeds a byte cap, naming its size, the cap and the section that
grew most; the cap is 042.1's landed size plus headroom, set when it is filed. A raise is a
reviewed one-line change, so growth becomes a decision. Open for filing: whether the *Testing
idiom* gets a cap of its own, and D3, which this sub-PRD's first weeks inform.

## 042.3 (outline): dedupe the budgets list and the Commands table

**What the human gets:** the same facts in fewer bytes, in the two places that repeat themselves.
Depends on nothing; files after 042.1 to avoid two concurrent edits of one graded unit.

- The budgets invariant (eleven budgets, each "never folded into the other N") becomes a table
  of knob, axis and default; the count sentence and ADR-0020's reason stay once.
- The Commands table drops rows that restate a `veri:` line; the `veri:` lines and every row
  `test_claude_md_commands.py` needs for a `.PHONY` target stay.
- Rows `CM35`–`CM39`, `CM83`, `CM176`, `CM250`, `CM300` and the Commands rows are rewritten to
  match; the unit is regraded.

## What this PRD does not hold

- **Excluding `CLAUDE.md` from leaves** (`claudeMdExcludes`): leaves need the test-writing
  contract and the `veri:` lines; PRD 024 already removed it from the grader (024.4).
- **The README's other sections** beyond KM8 and KM10's rows.
- **Spend.** No CI, runner or paid service change; the saving is per-request tokens, unmeasured
  here.
