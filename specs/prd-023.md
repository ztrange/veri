## The goal

`make test` takes a fraction of the 381–434 s it took on CI on 2026-11-10, and a conductor waits
less for the machine-wide full-run lock.

The target (2 s, Mario, 2026-11-10; `make unit` already has a 2 s target) is stated here in prose.
Acceptance criteria are behaviours only, and `make profile` before and after is quoted in each PR
body (CLAUDE.md, *`make test` on the development Mac*).

### What the 2 s goal implies

#3615 set out to reach 2 s for `make unit` and did not: the fixed cost every case pays is now
about 2 ms, and what is left is the cases' own bodies (about 65 % of CPU, mostly cases driving a
module's CLI in-process) and collection. A 2 s run therefore needs cases rewritten or moved to
another tier, or `make test` to mean "the cases this change can affect". Which of those is
intended is the first decision in *Open*.
