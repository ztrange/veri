# PRD 024 requirements: what a leaf costs before it does anything, and what green rejects cost

The format is `specs/utility-skills.md`'s: each row makes one claim a reviewer, a grader or a test
can mark true or false; IDs are stable, and a dropped row leaves a gap.

**Status: DRAFT, from the 2026-11-10 code audit. Outlines only: every sub-PRD waits on a
measurement or a decision. No decision has been put to Mario yet. Nothing is filed.**

## The goal

A leaf's fixed cost (what it writes to the cache before its first useful turn) falls, and fewer
green attempts are rejected. Both are measured before anything changes.

Target: stated per sub-PRD once measured. Acceptance criteria are behaviours only.

## What this PRD does not hold

PRD 012 (`specs/prd-012.md`, section 4) already owns:

- the conductor's cost and context (CR1, CR2) and its model knob (CR3);
- the revise budget (BG3) and refactor-pass reporting (BG4–BG6), including the finding that most
  refactor passes land nothing (72.7 % there; 35.6 % of steps by this audit's count).

The audit's first report said no metrics file counts the conductor. **That was wrong for recent
runs**: `metrics.py:530-555` prints `Conductor own spending` (for example
`.tdd/issue-3578-metrics.md`: $6.34 over 132 turns, on opus). Older reports do exclude it. The
auditor's own sum of 953 local `conductor-*.log` files (median $4.81 per issue, total $5,724, all
opus) agrees with PRD 012's "50–70 % of a run's cost" and adds nothing to it.

`VERI_SKIP_VERIFY=refactor` exists (PRD 018.1). Whether to turn it on is an operator setting, not
a requirement: verify-refactor rejected 37 of about 3,742 attempts (0.99 %).
