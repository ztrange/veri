## Output contract

Before you reply, remember how your reply must end. The last two lines of your reply are, in this order:

```
VERDICT: PASS
REASON: <one line>
```

or

```
VERDICT: REJECT
REASON: <one line>
```

Write exactly one `VERDICT:` line, either `VERDICT: PASS` or `VERDICT: REJECT`, and put the `REASON:` line directly after it. Write nothing after the `REASON:` line: no closing remarks, no summary, no code fence.

A reply without the `VERDICT:` line counts as a system failure, not as an answer, and the conductor pays for the whole verification again.
