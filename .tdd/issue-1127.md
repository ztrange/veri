# Plan for #1127 — VERI_CONDUCTOR_CTX_BUDGET with a leading zero is read as octal, and 08 never stops a conductor

Chosen behaviour: a leading-zero budget is read as **decimal** — `0100000` is 100000 tokens, `08` is 8 — in both launchers that read it (`orchestrate.sh`, `parallel-conductors.sh`). Nothing is refused that is accepted today.

- [x] orchestrate.sh with VERI_CONDUCTOR_CTX_BUDGET=0100000 reads the budget as decimal 100000: a conductor whose context is 50000 after its step-done call is not stopped, and one whose context is 100001 is; the octal 0100000 assertion in test_orchestrate.py's leading-zero test is replaced by this one ⟦criterion 1⟧
- [x] orchestrate.sh with VERI_CONDUCTOR_CTX_BUDGET=08 stops a conductor once its context is over 8 tokens after its step-done call, with no "value too great for base" line on stderr; the 08/09 never-stopping assertions in test_orchestrate.py's leading-zero test are replaced by this one ⟦criterion 2⟧
- [x] parallel-conductors.sh with VERI_CONDUCTOR_CTX_BUDGET=0100000 reads the budget as decimal 100000: a conductor whose context is 50000 after its step-done call is neither stopped nor relaunched; the 0100000-is-octal-32768 case in test_parallel_conductors.py is replaced by this one ⟦criterion 1⟧
- [x] parallel-conductors.sh with VERI_CONDUCTOR_CTX_BUDGET=08 stops and relaunches a conductor once its context is over 8 tokens after its step-done call, with no "value too great for base" line on stderr; the 08-never-stops and 09-never-stops cases in test_parallel_conductors.py are replaced by this one ⟦criterion 2⟧
- [ ] parallel-conductors.sh with VERI_CONDUCTOR_CTX_BUDGET=09 stops and relaunches a conductor once its context is over 9 tokens after its step-done call, with no "value too great for base" line on stderr ⟦added⟧ ⟦criterion 2⟧
- [ ] with VERI_CONDUCTOR_CTX_BUDGET=100000, and with it unset (default 120000), orchestrate.sh and parallel-conductors.sh stop a conductor exactly when its context after a step-done call is over that number, as today ⟦criterion 3⟧
