### `tests-frozen.sh rerecord-rebased <ledger> <pre-rebase-sha>` re-records an upstream-only stale case: on a branch whose ledger holds a ticked earlier step with a frozen case (recorded by `record --step`), where that case was then changed only on `origin/<default>` (the branch `refs/remotes/origin/HEAD` names) and the branch was rebased onto it, the verb rewrites that earlier step's record at the case's `HEAD` body and exits 0, and the next `check-earlier` for a later step exits 0; the module's usage line names the new verb.
