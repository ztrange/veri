### veri.next_pending_refactor.main drops NUL bytes from stdin and prints a non-UTF-8 byte in a step's text back as that same byte ⟦criterion 6⟧
- the stdin read (`sys.stdin.buffer.read().replace(b"\0", b"").decode(errors="surrogateescape")`), the first-match loop and the `surrogateescape` write in `veri/next_pending_refactor.py:main` are the same code as in `veri/next_unchecked.py:main`, and the read is also in `veri/ledger_steps.py:main`. Merging them would mean a new shared helper, and the spec rules that out for this port ("veri/ledger.py gains nothing (no `first_step` helper…)"). If it is done at all, it belongs in a follow-up once both ports have landed.
- `refactor_pending` repeats the done-and-pending check `r[1] == "done" and r[3] == "1"` that is written inline in `veri/ledger_steps.py:current`. Merging the two would mean importing across modules, which is out of scope here.
- test-only | in `tests/py/test_next_pending_refactor.py`, the steps "fake stdin, call `main`, collect `(code, *capsysbinary.readouterr())`" repeat in every case. They could become one helper next to `fake_stdin`.
