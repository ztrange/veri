- [ ] `mutation-probe.sh <file> <old> <new> -- <command…>` replaces the literal text `<old>` with `<new>` in `<file>` without `sed` and runs `<command…>` against the edited file, exiting 0 when the command fails under the mutation; a case in `skills/tdd/scripts/tests/py/test_mutation_probe_process.py` runs the probe over a fixture file on the host it runs on and observes that the command saw the edited content (behaviour, never the string of a command) ⟦criterion 1⟧

- [ ] `mutation-probe.sh` exits 1, never 0, when `<command…>` still passes under the mutation, naming on stderr that the suite stayed green under the mutation ⟦criterion 3⟧
- [ ] Whatever `mutation-probe.sh` exits — 0, 1 or 2 — `<file>` is byte-identical to what it was before the probe and no saved copy of it is left beside it ⟦criterion 4⟧

- [ ] A case in `skills/tdd/scripts/tests/py/unit/test_mutation_probe_commands.py` greps every template under `skills/tdd/scripts/prompts/` and the source of `veri/mutation_probe.py` and finds no `sed -i` without a backup suffix ⟦criterion 5⟧
